# Authoring content

The content pack (`content/`) is plain JSON; the loader identifies every file by its `schema` field.

```
content/
  standards/standards.json        acucraft/standards@1   point-location editions and their known differences
  acupoints/<channel>.json        acucraft/acupoints@1   the point library, one file per channel (409 points; 399 localised),
                                                         generated by tools/content/acupoints.py
  regions/*.json                  acucraft/region@1      regional models (18, the whole body) — docs/ANATOMY.md
  regions/body-map.json           acucraft/body-map@1    where each chart lies on the body (what is in reach from beside
                                                         the couch) — docs/WORLD.md
  sg/roles.json                   acucraft/roles@1       professional roles and permissions
  sg/ruleset-*.json               acucraft/ruleset@1     versioned rule sets with effective dates
  sg/clinic.json                  acucraft/clinic@1      the simulated clinic: its facility, each room's home spot, the
                                                         placement every fixture stands on
  world/facilities/*.json         clinicalcraft/facility@1          the clinic's floor plan, in ClinicalCraft's formats —
  world/templates/*.json          clinicalcraft/room-template@1     written with sg/clinic.json by tools/content/clinic_world.py;
  world/catalogs/*.json           clinicalcraft/equipment-catalog@1 docs/WORLD.md
  sg/vocabulary.json              acucraft/vocabulary@1  diagnoses, patterns, red flags (assessment ids); the signs, what
                                                         each pattern is recognised by, the needle sensations and deqi —
                                                         written by tools/content/vocabulary.py; docs/EVIDENCE.md
  sg/evidence.json                acucraft/evidence@1    guideline recommendations, with versions
  families/<id>/family.json       acucraft/family@1      a case family: title, condition, split, variants
  families/<id>/case.json         clinicalcraft/case@1   the consultation (history, exam, records, physiology)
  families/<id>/field.json        acucraft/field@1       the acupuncture field and the hidden evaluation
  families/<id>/variants/*.json   acucraft/variant@1     overlays of case and field
  families/<id>/solutions/*.json                         a variant's reference trajectory (hidden)
  bench/pilot-v1.json             acucraft/suite@1       the pilot benchmark suite
```

## Families and variants

A family is one patient and presentation; each variant changes one thing on purpose and says so (`purpose`, hidden
from agents): `base`, `risk`, `anatomy`, `position`, `event`, `red-flag`, `wording`, `follow-up`. A variant overlays the
family's `case` and `field` with id-aware merge patches (`engine/util.ts` `applyPatch`):

- objects merge key by key; `null` deletes a key;
- an array of objects with `id`, patched with such an array, merges item by item: same id merges, `"$remove": true`
  deletes, a new id appends;
- `{"$replace": value}` replaces whatever is there;
- anything else replaces.

The underlying patient state stays fixed and versioned (`family.version`).

## The case (`case.json`)

A ClinicalCraft case (`docs/CASE_AUTHORING.md` in ClinicalCraft). AcuCraft conventions:

- phases `visit-1` and `follow-up`; the follow-up's `enterWhen` is `{"flag": "acu.visit-2"}`;
- a simulated clock;
- existing reports as `performedAt: "records"` investigations with `availability.historical`;
- physiology events may listen to the `acu.*` flags (`acu.vasovagal`, `acu.lying`, `acu.needles-in`);
- the last objective completes on `{"flag": "acu.finished"}`; `solution` is ClinicalCraft's own walkthrough (checked by
  `verifyCase`).

## The field (`field.json`)

- `clinic`, `roles`, `ruleSeries`, `visits` (index, phase, date, label), `cast` (who is under test; NPCs and their
  policy), `regions` modelled in the scenario.
- `patient`: location, position, risk factors (matched against point cautions and against `avoid-regions` rules),
  `gestationWeeks` (with `pregnancy`), anxiety, `needleSensitivity` (how readily they feel a needle: scales the
  needle's response and what they tolerate), positions declined, anatomy
  (finger cun; per region `both`/`left`/`right`: scale, subcutaneous and muscle scale, landmark and structure shifts),
  site conditions (circle or whole region, revealed by an exam finding), Ashi points (revealed by an exam finding),
  consent (required topics; agree, questions, decline texts).
- `equipment.packs`, `safety.guard`.
- `events`: `when` (a field condition), `afterMinutes`, seeded `probability`, `interrupts`, severity and effects
  (ClinicalCraft flags, field flags, speech, patient feedback, consent withdrawal, a needle dropping out or breaking,
  anxiety).
- `evaluation`: pathways (acceptable or not), expected and acceptable diagnoses, defensible and implausible patterns,
  `signs` (what each finding of the case shows, below), red flags present or absent (each with `evident` — shown by
  the opening statement or triage note — and `evidence`, the conditions that give the learner it), points recommended /
  acceptable / to avoid (a group point's entries may name a `site`), critical errors, required history, exam and
  records, monitoring limits, follow-up pathways, documentation fields. A pathway may say it `continues` (calls for
  another visit); full-course completion is counted only then. Hidden from agents.

### What the findings show (`evaluation.signs`)

A pattern differentiation names the findings it rests on, and is read from what those findings show
([EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md)). `signs` is the scenario's reading of its own case: evidence id → sign ids of the
vocabulary. The id is a history item's or an examination finding's own id, or `<investigation>.<phase>` for a record.

```json
"signs": { "hx-weather": ["pain-worse-cold-damp", "pain-better-warmth", "heaviness"], "ex-tongue": ["tongue-pale", "coat-white-greasy"] }
```

A variant that changes a finding changes what it shows (`"signs": {"hx-swelling": ["joint-hot-red-swollen"]}`; `[]`
for a finding that no longer shows anything). Resolving a scenario checks the mapping (findings of the case, signs of
the vocabulary) and holds the pattern lists to it: every `defensible` pattern must be supportable from the case's own
findings — enough key signs, enough signs in all, not contradicted — and no `implausible` pattern may be. A case's
history, examination and record ids must be distinct, and none may look like an id the evidence ledger numbers itself
(`vitals-1`, `obs-1`, `sense-1`, `feel-1`).

### Field conditions

`all`, `any`, `not`, `always`; `clinical` (any ClinicalCraft condition: `asked`, `examined`, `read`, `flag`, `vital` …);
`flag`; `needled {point|ashi, side?}`; `needlesInserted`, `needlesRetained` (ranges); `needlesResolved`;
`disposition (visit?)`; `referred {to?, urgency?}`; `pattern`; `diagnosed`; `redFlag`; `consent`; `explained (visit?)`;
`position`; `positioned`; `unsafe`; `violation`; `feedback`; `event`; `respondedTo {event, withinMinutes}`;
`withdrawnAfter {event, withinMinutes}`; `positionedAfter {event, position, withinMinutes}` (the position at that time); `stopped`; `documented`; `visit`; `visitEnded`; `task`; `elapsed`.

## Reference trajectories and authoring scripts

Each variant's reference trajectory (`solutions/<variant>.json`) must reach an acceptable pathway with no rejection
and no critical error (`npm run validate`). Families after the first are written by scripts in `tools/content/` (one per
family, sharing `lib.py`) that emit the case, field, variants and solutions together; `knee_solutions.py` writes the
first family's trajectories.

A script says what the reference practitioner does; `lib.py`'s `Trajectory` and `tools/finish-solutions.ts` supply what
follows from it:

- **walking** — the practitioner has a body ([WORLD.md](WORLD.md)). The helper keeps track of where they stand and
  emits the `move` each act needs (to the sink, the shelf, the trolley, the patient's side, the side of the couch a
  point is on); needles queued with `needle()` are picked up together and put in one side of the couch at a time;
- **the evidence of a differentiation** — an `assess` with a pattern names its findings (`patternEvidence`), ids of
  what the script asked and examined before it;
- **the needle's response** — `needle(..., work="lift-thrust" | "rotate", ask=True)` works a needle once it is in and
  asks the patient what they feel at it; `work()` and `feel()` do the same later;
- **finishing** — `write_family` runs `tools/finish-solutions.ts`, which replays each trajectory on the engine, drops
  the walks that covered no ground, writes each treatment record's `needling.responses` from the evidence the
  trajectory obtained (deqi where it shows it, none where it does not, not-sought where nothing was looked for), and
  replays the result strictly.

Mark coordinates come from

```bash
npm run acu -- locate sg-knee-oa/base supine-knee-flexed ST35:right ST36:right
```

which prints the hidden patient-specific instances. Then:

```bash
npm run validate
```

```bash
npx vitest run
```

The tests also check that the base trajectory **fails** on every other variant of its family: a variant that does not
change the outcome of the base course is not testing anything.

## Review status

Every file that carries clinical, anatomical or regulatory content has a `review` (or `provenance.review`) with
`unreviewed`, `editorial` or `expert-reviewed`, and sources marked `verified: false` until checked. Everything in this
release is `unreviewed`.
