# Response to the clinical and gameplay audit (November 2026)

The audit compared main at `cb6a25a` with the design proposals, the runnable cases and the TCMPB guidance. This page
records what changed in response, finding by finding, and what remains open. Each counterexample the audit ran is now a
regression test in `packages/bench/src/audit.test.ts`.

## Priority findings

| finding | change | test |
|---|---|---|
| Pregnancy, 24 weeks: the reference needled a right L3 Ashi point and passed | The 2021 advisory's §4.1.4 is a rule (`avoid-regions`): after the first trimester no needle in the abdomen, lumbar region or sacrum and buttocks, judged by region — a named point, an Ashi point and a bare chart position alike — as the critical unsafe act `insertion-in-avoided-region`. The variant records `gestationWeeks`, resolves to deferral with obstetric liaison (`p-defer-obstetric`), lists lumbosacral needling as unacceptable and expects no follow-up visit. The protocol agent reads the rule from the library and defers. | "the original lumbar needling pathway fails"; "judges the region …"; "the reviewed deferral … completes the case"; "does not apply in the first trimester" |
| Reversed ST35 and EX-LE4 marks scored 0.01 cm and 1.00 | Each insertion records the point meant (named or marked), the point the entry lies on, and the attribution the rules use. Localisation error is measured from the point meant; an identity item says whether the entry lies on it; a point to avoid is avoided wherever the needle went. | "reversing the marks of ST35 and EX-LE4 gives two errors" |

## Clinical reasoning and scoring

| finding | change | test |
|---|---|---|
| Disposition and referral alone met the primary endpoint in the four red-flag variants | The primary endpoint stays the disposition (timely referral keeps its credit). Reports add separate outcomes: red flags recognised on evidence the learner had, the first visit, and the planned course. Red flags carry `evident` (the opening statement or triage note shows it) and `evidence` (a question asked or volunteered, an examination, a report read); suite reports show each proportion. | "disposition and referral alone keep the disposition but show no red flag recognised on evidence"; "a red flag shown at the outset is evidence enough to refer at once" |
| Consent from topic tags without an explanation | An explanation covers only what is said: tags without words explain nothing, and the words are recorded. | "topic tags without words explain nothing" |
| A change from no stimulation to a reducing technique carried consent forward | A change of method or stimulation (other than to none) is material: consent no longer covers the plan. | "a change of stimulation … needs a further discussion" |
| Palpating a prepared site again went unflagged | Handling cleaned skin (palpating a landmark, measuring, marking) marks its preparation as touched: insertion there is `site-not-prepared` until it is cleaned again. | "palpating a prepared site again undoes its preparation" |
| Ending the knee course after visit 1 met the primary endpoint | The first visit and the planned course are reported apart; the course counts only where the pathway calls for another visit. | "ending a course … completes the initial visit, not the course" |

## Gameplay and teamwork

| finding | change | test |
|---|---|---|
| A count from reception reconciled needles in the treatment room | Counts record how they were made: `observed` where the needles are (the only kind that reconciles), `read` from elsewhere, or `reported` — a colleague's bedside count received through a delegated task, with who counted. | "a count from reception reads the record …" |
| Moving with the patient set them seated without updating the marks | The move runs the same position change as `position_patient`: marks made lying down go stale once the patient sits. | "marks made with the knee extended need rechecking …" |
| An assistant could report the couch ready without doing anything | `prepare_couch` (surface cleaned, fresh linen) records who and when; couch state is in the agent view; the task completes only when it was done. | "a delegated couch preparation is done, recorded and visible" |

## Still open

- **The clinic.** There is no live, navigable 3D clinic. Movement within a room — approaching the couch, a patient
  falling behind, an obstructed route, assisted arrival at the bedside — is not modelled. The end-to-end right-knee
  episode the audit describes is not built as a scene: discomfort, team response and a bedside debrief exist only in
  the event log. Humans and agents do share the same actions, objects, permissions and event record.
- **Pattern differentiation and deqi.** The last TCM pattern submitted is still checked against a list. Sensations are
  still scripted from the tissue at the tip. Linking the four examinations, the learner's actions and the patient's
  response as evidence is not done.
- **Review.** All 409 point definitions, every regional model and every rule encoding remain unreviewed. Before any
  assessment use:
  - verify the high-risk regions first: the source, the patient's position, the structures at risk and the test
    conditions;
  - check the 2021 rules clause by clause against the advisory's text;
  - obtain and encode the 2027 advisory.
- **Anatomy.** The trunk comes from one male reference body; the head and limbs are schematic. The model shows the
  hazards it encodes; safe limits in patients need independent validation (docs/ANATOMY.md).
- **Benchmark.** The pilot suite is one seed and two held-out families, written by the same authors as the agents
  (docs/EVALUATION.md §7). Independent cases and clinician review come next.
